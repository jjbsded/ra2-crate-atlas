本 Toy 版为低清简化版，非完整版；地图底图细节与功能范围均有精简。

This Toy build is a low-resolution simplified edition, not the full application. Background detail and the feature set are reduced.

网页版包含官方地图的预计算初始布局，收录的模式、地图及版本以地图列表为准。结果使用与本地工具相同的精确静态模型，包含最多 1000 轮重抽，不模拟游戏进程、后续资源生长、触发或开局生成的单位。

This edition contains precomputed official maps; see the map list for included modes, maps and game versions. It uses the desktop edition’s exact static model, including up to 1,000 attempts. It does not simulate a running game, later ore growth, triggers or newly spawned starting units.

1× 是只从自身起点落箱、没有邻格转入的基准；来源百分比是假定从该来源格开始，一次附近搜索转入选中格的概率，不计失败后重抽。

1× is the no-incoming-transfer baseline. Origin percentages give the chance of reaching the selected cell with that origin fixed, excluding retries.

单指拖动平移，轻点格子查看来源，双指缩放和平移。区域编辑时可以切换「触控：移动／画笔」，双指始终用于导航。区域按地图标识保存在当前浏览器，可导出划分备份；更换设备或清除网站数据不会自动迁移。

Drag with one finger to pan, tap a cell to inspect origins, and pinch to zoom. In region mode, choose Pan or Brush; two fingers always navigate. Region selections are saved in this browser and can be exported/restored. They do not automatically sync across devices.

矿石/树木开关读取预计算的“保留矿石和树木／移除矿石和树木”布局，计入两者对概率的影响。图片导出保留区域及概率数字。自定义地图、Mod、原贴图精细 3D 和 MP4 导出请使用本地完整版。

The Ore/Trees switch loads separate precomputed layouts with preset ore / gems and trees retained or removed. Ore drills and other scenery remain. Image exports retain regions and readable multiplier labels. Custom maps, Mods, detailed original-art 3D and MP4 export require the desktop edition.

选中格子后点击「3D 查看」，按当前预计算布局生成附近地形，默认 21×21，最大 31×31。保留高度、坡形、桥上／桥下、相关高格及禁行判定标识；概率默认关闭，可在视角与图层中打开。外观沿用逐格简绘素材，不是原游戏的精细模型。单指拖动旋转、双指平移缩放、轻点选格；电脑也可右键拖动平移。手机上展开「视角与图层」和「格子信息与判定」可查看完整选项。需要浏览器支持 WebGL；3D 仅在点击时加载。

Select a cell and choose “3D view” to inspect its precomputed surroundings: 21×21 cells by default, up to 31×31. Heights, slopes, bridge decks and ground underneath, related high cells and rule markers are retained. Probability starts off; enable it under View and layers. Artwork uses simplified cell materials, not detailed game models. Drag to rotate, use two fingers to pan/zoom, and tap to select; a mouse also supports right-drag panning. On phones, expand View and layers or Cell details and rules for options. WebGL is required; 3D loads only when opened.

50MB 轻量包保留全部作战／小队联盟地图。默认按原始高度与坡形逐格拼接共享简化素材：方块悬崖、简单地表纹理及建筑／单位符号；油井、修桥小屋等有专属符号。放大与导出会重新绘制，不放大一张低清底图。简化外形只作示意，选格、通行及遮挡标识仍以原地图数据为准。精确分数、地形和来源数据采用无损编码。

The 50 MB package retains every Battle and Team Alliance map. Shared materials are assembled per cell at the original heights and slopes: block cliffs, simple ground textures and object symbols, with dedicated icons for oil derricks and repair huts. Zooming and exports redraw these shapes rather than magnifying a small bitmap. Shapes are illustrative; picking, passability and occlusion markers still use the original map data. Exact fractions, terrain and origin records are losslessly encoded.

区域编辑的「连通填充」只合并同区域、同海陆类型、沿格边连通的部分；同区域的孤立部分保留。可撤销／重做，拖动不触发填充。

Connected fill merges only edge-connected cells with the same source region and land/sea type. Isolated parts remain unchanged. Undo/redo are supported; dragging does not fill.

自动划分可设置玩家队伍 A／B／C／D／中立／空，默认中立玩家各自独立。同队共享区域并可互相通行，空位不占地。修改后重新自动划分生效，可撤销。统计模式下，编号下方色块标明对应区域及队伍，空位显示半透明红叉；区域中心显示名称和总概率，统一使用外白内黑双层描边，区域名字号为概率的 70%、垂直居中。

Automatic partition supports teams A/B/C/D, Neutral and Empty. Neutral players are independent; teammates share territory without blocking one another, and empty slots claim nothing. Rerun to apply changes; undo is supported. In region mode, squares below player numbers show region colors and team letters; empty slots fade with a red X. Center labels include region names and total probabilities, with white outer / black inner outlines; names use 70% of the probability size and are vertically centered.
