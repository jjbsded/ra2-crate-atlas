本 Toy 版为轻量网页版，非完整版。原版贴图版本使用无损共享图集；旧版使用简绘素材，以地图页面标示为准。

This is the lightweight web edition, not the full application. Original-art builds use lossless shared atlases; older builds use simplified artwork. The map page identifies the active edition.

网页版包含官方地图的预计算初始布局，收录的模式、地图及版本以地图列表为准。结果使用与本地工具相同的精确静态模型，不模拟游戏进程、后续资源生长、触发或开局生成的单位。

This edition contains precomputed official maps; see the map list for included modes, maps and game versions. It uses the desktop edition’s exact static model, using one origin draw and placement check. It does not simulate a running game, later ore growth, triggers or newly spawned starting units.

1× 是只从自身起点落箱、没有邻格转入的基准；来源百分比是假定从该来源格开始，一次附近搜索转入选中格的概率。

1× is the no-incoming-transfer baseline. Origin percentages give the chance of reaching the selected cell with that origin fixed.

单指拖动平移，轻点格子查看来源，双指缩放和平移。区域编辑时可以切换「触控：移动／画笔」，双指始终用于导航。区域按地图标识保存在当前浏览器，可导出划分备份；更换设备或清除网站数据不会自动迁移。

Drag with one finger to pan, tap a cell to inspect origins, and pinch to zoom. In region mode, choose Pan or Brush; two fingers always navigate. Region selections are saved in this browser and can be exported/restored. They do not automatically sync across devices.

模拟放置与增量方案由最终 Toy 的浏览器计算模块提供，操作见其帮助页。旧矿石／树木开关已移除。 / The final Toy includes browser-local simulation and delta plans. The former Ore/Trees switch has been removed.

选中格子后点击「3D 查看」，按当前预计算布局生成附近地形，默认 21×21，最大 31×31。保留高度、坡形、桥上／桥下、相关高格及禁行判定标识；概率默认关闭，可在视角与图层中打开。原版贴图构建复用游戏地表和静态对象图像，旧版使用简绘素材；悬崖几何仍为简化模型。单指拖动旋转、双指平移缩放、轻点选格；电脑也可右键拖动平移。手机上展开「视角与图层」和「格子信息与判定」可查看完整选项。需要浏览器支持 WebGL；3D 仅在点击时加载。

Select a cell and choose “3D view” to inspect its precomputed surroundings: 21×21 cells by default, up to 31×31. Heights, slopes, bridge decks and ground underneath, related high cells and rule markers are retained. Probability starts off; enable it under View and layers. Original-art builds reuse game terrain and static sprites; legacy builds use simplified materials. Cliff geometry remains simplified. Drag to rotate, use two fingers to pan/zoom, and tap to select; a mouse also supports right-drag panning. On phones, expand View and layers or Cell details and rules for options. WebGL is required; 3D loads only when opened.

50MB 轻量包保留全部作战／小队联盟地图。原版贴图版逐格拼接游戏原始像素，共用无损图集，按当前地图加载，保留桥梁遮挡与阴影。大小地图、区域裁图、3D 和导出共用这套素材；放大与导出不依赖低清整图。3D 悬崖仍为简化几何，建筑和单位为静态贴图，不包含全套动画或完整游戏模型。精确分数、地形和来源数据采用无损编码。

The 50 MB package retains every Battle and Team Alliance map. Original-art builds assemble the game’s native pixels cell by cell from lossless shared atlases loaded per map, preserving bridge occlusion and shadows. The main map, minimap, region crops, 3D and exports reuse these assets instead of a reduced whole-map bitmap. 3D cliffs use simplified geometry and objects use static sprites, not full animation sets or game models. Exact fractions, terrain and origin records are losslessly encoded.

区域编辑的「连通填充」只合并同区域、同海陆类型、沿格边连通的部分；同区域的孤立部分保留。可撤销／重做，拖动不触发填充。

Connected fill merges only edge-connected cells with the same source region and land/sea type. Isolated parts remain unchanged. Undo/redo are supported; dragging does not fill.

自动划分可设置玩家队伍 A／B／C／D／中立／空，默认中立玩家各自独立。同队共享区域并可互相通行，空位不占地。修改后重新自动划分生效，可撤销。统计模式下，编号下方色块标明对应区域及队伍，空位显示半透明红叉；区域中心显示名称和总概率，统一使用外白内黑双层描边，区域名字号为概率的 70%、垂直居中。

Automatic partition supports teams A/B/C/D, Neutral and Empty. Neutral players are independent; teammates share territory without blocking one another, and empty slots claim nothing. Rerun to apply changes; undo is supported. In region mode, squares below player numbers show region colors and team letters; empty slots fade with a red X. Center labels include region names and total probabilities, with white outer / black inner outlines; names use 70% of the probability size and are vertically centered.
